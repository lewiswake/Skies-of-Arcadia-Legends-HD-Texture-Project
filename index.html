v0.3.0

The Complete 100% ISO Remaster

November 9, 2026

Version 0.3.0 achieves 100% complete coverage across all 31,010 textures extracted directly from the internal .mld and .mlk archives of the GameCube disc image. Converted into Microsoft DirectX BC7 DDS with hardware mipmaps, audited for low-alpha ghost fringing, and remastered with standardized atmospheric alpha smoothing.

Changes in v0.3.0:

  • 100% GameCube ISO Coverage (31,010 Textures): Full 4x upscale and curation across all game files dumped directly from the raw disc image.
  • Alpha Channel & Ghost Haze Audit: Audited all 30,000+ textures to eliminate low-alpha edge fringing (1 ≤ α ≤ 25), removing gray bounding-box outlines on character cutouts and foliage.
  • Standardized Atmospheric FX Pipeline: Transparent overlays (clouds, smoke, mist, fog, and heat haze) upscaled with proportional Gaussian blur strictly on the alpha channel to preserve soft gradients without pixel stair-stepping.
  • Scene Corrections: Resolved Mid-Ocean Rhaknam cutscene fog blowout, purged 7 solid-opaque hash-colliding textures in Temple of Pyrynn Area 2, repaired Galcian eye/cape alpha masks, and standardized the Delphinus Bridge flag shadow.
  • Runtime GPU Textures: Extracted and remastered dynamic Format 8 (C4, 4-bit paletted) textures generated during endgame bosses, ship battles, and Intro Movie A with dual _arb.dds compatibility aliases.

How to Install on Dolphin Emulator

Follow these steps to correctly apply the textures and ensure zero-stutter 60 FPS performance.

  1. Extract the files

    Download your desired .7z packages from the GitHub Releases page and extract them using 7-Zip (Windows) or The Unarchiver (macOS). Inside you will find the GEA folder.

  2. Move to Dolphin's Load Directory

    Copy the extracted GEA folder directly into Dolphin's texture directory:

    OS Directory Locations:
    • Windows: Documents\Dolphin Emulator\Load\Textures\GEA
    • macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Dolphin/Load/Textures/GEA
    • Linux: ~/.var/app/org.DolphinEmu.dolphin-emu/data/dolphin-emu/Load/Textures/GEA (Flatpak) or ~/.local/share/dolphin-emu/Load/Textures/GEA

  3. Enable Custom Textures in Dolphin

    Open Dolphin and click on Graphics, then navigate to the Advanced tab. Under the Utility section, check Load Custom Textures.

  4. Enable Prefetching (Crucial for Stutter-Free Play)

    Under Load Custom Textures, check Prefetch Custom Textures. Because all files in this project are optimized as native BC7 DDS, prefetching streams them directly into your GPU's VRAM upon boot, eliminating in-game hitching and micro-stutters entirely.

    Recommended: Under Graphics → General, ensure Backend is set to Vulkan or Direct3D 12. Under Enhancements, check Arbitrary Mipmap Detection and uncheck Disable Fog.

The Upscale Methodology

All textures are meticulously curated and upscaled 4x from raw GameCube ISO files:

UI & Fonts

Reconstructed and redrawn with clean vector geometry for razor-sharp 1440p and 4K display.

Characters & Faces

Airbrushed and enhanced in Photoshop to preserve original expressive hand-drawn anime characteristics while removing compression noise.

Weapons & Equipment

Party weapons (cutlasses, boomerangs, Cupil forms, arm cannons, and rapiers) are upscaled 4x with Topaz Gigapixel AI 'High fidelity', followed by manual Photoshop hand-painting to reconstruct metallic blade bevels, reflections, leather wraps, and crystal finishes.

Environment

Environment assets are upscaled using Topaz Gigapixel AI's 'Recover' generative model.

Special Effects Pipeline

Special effect textures (clouds, smoke, fire, shadows, lighting) are upscaled 4x with Gaussian blur applied strictly to the alpha channel to eliminate harsh pixelation without gradient loss.
